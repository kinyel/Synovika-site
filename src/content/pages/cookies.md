## What this site stores

**Your motion setting.** At the foot of every page you can choose full or reduced motion. If you do, your browser remembers the choice in its local storage, under the name `synovika:motion`, so the next page you open respects it. It stays on your device, we never see it, and you can clear it at any time from your browser's settings.

That's all this website stores itself.

## Our hosting provider

The site is delivered by Cloudflare. To protect the site from attacks and automated traffic, Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary security cookie in some cases. It isn't used for advertising or to follow you around the web.

## What we don't use

- No analytics cookies.
- No advertising or retargeting cookies.
- No social media tracking pixels.

If that ever changes, we'll ask you first, and we'll update this notice.
