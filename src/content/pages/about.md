## Built around reality

Our tagline is a promise about where software should start. Not from a template, and not from a list of features someone saw elsewhere, but from how your business really works: the roads your drivers take, the gate your guard opens, the page your team needs to change tonight.

That's why we spend so long listening before we build anything, and why we test on the phones and networks your customers actually use.

## Two kinds of work

**Our own products.** We make software for problems we keep seeing. [Selvion](/products/selvion/) is our fleet tracking platform. The [Estate Entrance Security System](/products/estate-entrance-security/) brings code-based visitor access to residential estates. [Websites with an Admin Panel](/products/websites-with-admin-panel/) let your team run its own website. All three are in development.

**Your software.** We [build new systems](/services/software-development/), [fix software that's broken](/services/fixing-and-debugging/), [test it properly](/services/testing-and-qa/), [write down what it must do](/services/requirements-engineering/), [plan how it fits together](/services/system-architecture/) and [look after it](/services/maintenance-and-support/) for years after launch.
