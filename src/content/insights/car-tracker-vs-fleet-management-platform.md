"We just need a tracker" is how many fleet conversations start. Sometimes that's true. Often, what the business really needs is what a tracker makes possible: knowing what every vehicle is doing without making a single phone call. That's the difference between a car tracker and a fleet management platform.

## What a car tracker does

A car tracker is a small GPS device fitted in a vehicle. It works out where the vehicle is from satellite signals and sends that position over the mobile network, usually to an app or a website. With one, you can:

- see where the vehicle is now;
- see where it has been;
- often get an alert if it moves when it shouldn't.

For one personal car, or a single vehicle you mostly worry about being stolen, that can be all you need.

## What a fleet management platform adds

A fleet management platform takes the positions from trackers in every vehicle and turns them into a way of running the fleet:

- **One live map** of every vehicle, with its speed and when it last reported.
- **Alerts that come to you:** an engine starting, a vehicle leaving a zone, a vehicle sitting idle. Nobody has to sit and watch a map.
- **Geofences:** zones around depots, customers' sites or routes, with alerts when a vehicle crosses them. See [geofences explained](/insights/geofences-explained/).
- **Trip reports:** each journey written up, with distance, time, stops and top speed.
- **History you can search,** for disputes, fuel questions and planning.
- **Several users,** so dispatchers, managers and owners each see what they need.

## How to choose

Ask yourself these questions:

1. **How many vehicles does the business depend on?** One or two, a tracker may do. Several, and the time spent checking each one adds up fast.
2. **Who needs to know, and when?** If the answer is "the manager, the moment something happens", you need alerts, not just a map.
3. **Do you need records?** Trip reports and history settle arguments about hours, routes and fuel.
4. **What happens when a vehicle is taken?** Some platforms can immobilise a vehicle remotely, through a relay fitted with the tracker. Ask how it's kept safe and who is allowed to use it.
5. **How will you check it?** Most managers will use it on a phone, often on mobile data. Try it there.

## Questions to ask any supplier

- Which trackers does it work with, and who installs them?
- What happens when a vehicle loses signal?
- Can you export your data whenever you want?
- What's the full monthly cost per vehicle, including data and support?
